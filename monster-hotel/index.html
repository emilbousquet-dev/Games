How to Play

Guests arrive at the front desk. Check them in, then bring them what they ask for.
Happy guests = ★★★★★ reviews. Grumpy guests = bad reviews!

WASD walk
Mouse look around
Shift run
E or Click use / give / talk
Hold E clean a mess
Space your boss power
G drop what you carry
Esc pause

🍹 Food & potions are in the Kitchen · 🧻 Towels, bandages, lightning jars & bone polish are in the Supplies room
🟢 green lamp = room is free · 🔴 red = someone is staying · 🟠 orange = room is dirty, clean it!

🎮 Controllers work too!

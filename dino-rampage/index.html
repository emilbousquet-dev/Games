You're a baby T-Rex in a big city. Eat snacks, smash stuff, and grow into a GIANT! 🦖

How to Play

You start as a tiny baby T-Rex. Walk into small things to SMASH them, and eat the sparkly snacks 🍉🍩🍕 to grow faster!
Every time you grow, you can smash bigger things: cars 🚗 → houses 🏠 → shops 🏬 → SKYSCRAPERS 🏙️
Become a GIANT and fill the rampage bar to win!

📱 Left thumb: joystick to walk (push it far to RUN)
👆 Right thumb: swipe to look around
🦷 BITE · 🌀 TAIL whip · ⬆️ JUMP (jump again in the air = BELLY FLOP) · 🦖 ROAR when the circle is full

# SIGMA HOVER GP Σ🏍️⚡

A **3D hover racing game** like Mario Kart, that runs in your web browser!
Race hover karts, hover bikes, sky boards and even a **baby buggy** on **16 crazy tracks**,
throw rockets and banana slimes, drift to get purple sparks, and win the trophy. 🏆

Play **alone**, with a **friend on the same computer** (split screen), or **online with up to 4 players**.

## How to play

1. Open **`hover-gp/index.html`** in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (just double-click it).
2. Press **START**, pick a mode, pick your racer, build your hover ride, and GO!
3. Turn the sound on: every world has its own song, and the music gets faster on the **FINAL LAP**. 🔊

## Controls

| | 1 player | Player 1 (split screen) | Player 2 (split screen) | 🎮 Controller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | `←` `→` or `A` `D` | `A` `D` | `←` `→` | Stick or D-pad |
| Gas | automatic! (or `↑` / `W`) | automatic (or `W`) | automatic (or `↑`) | A |
| Brake / back up | `↓` or `S` | `S` | `↓` | B |
| Hop + **DRIFT** | `SPACE` or `SHIFT` (hold) | `SPACE` | `RIGHT SHIFT` or `/` | RB |
| Use item | `E`, `Q`, `J` or `ENTER` | `E` or `Q` | `ENTER` or `.` | LB or X |
| Throw item backwards | hold `↓` + item | hold `S` + item | hold `↓` + item | stick down + item |
| Look behind you | `C` | `C` | `,` | Y |
| Pause | `ESC` or `P` | `ESC` | `ESC` | START |

Menus: arrows + `ENTER` (and `ESC` to go back), a controller (D-pad + A + B), or the mouse.

**Auto gas** and **smart steering** (it stops you falling off the road) are ON at the start.
You can turn them off in **SETTINGS** when you're a pro.

## Tricks (like a pro!)

- **DRIFT**: hold the drift button while you turn. Sparks go **BLUE** → **ORANGE** → **PURPLE**.
  Let go for a **MINI-TURBO**. Purple = the biggest boost!
- **RAMP TRICK**: press drift just when you fly off a ramp. Your racer does a flip, and you get a boost when you land.
- **ROCKET START**: during the countdown, hold `↑` (or drift) right after the **"2"**. Too early and your engine stalls!
- **SLIPSTREAM**: drive right behind another racer for a moment and you get sucked along. Whoosh!
- **COINS**: every coin makes you a bit faster (up to 10). Getting hit makes you drop some.
- **BOOST PADS**: the orange arrows on the road.
- **GLIDE RAMPS**: the big blue ramps open your wings. Steer while you fly!
- **ANTI-GRAVITY**: on loops and twisty roads (blue glowing edges) your hover sticks to the road. Bump into racers there for a spin boost!

## Modes

- **GRAND PRIX** 🏆: race a cup of 4 tracks. You get points for every race (15 for 1st place).
  Finish in the top 3 to win a trophy. Speeds: **50cc**, **100cc**, **150cc**, **200cc** and **MIRROR** (everything is flipped!).
- **VS RACE** 🏁: pick any track, how many CPUs, how hard they are, items on/off, and how many laps.
- **BALLOON BATTLE** 🎈: everybody has 3 balloons. Hit people with items to pop theirs. Lose all 3 and you're out!
- **TIME TRIAL** ⏱️: race alone with 3 turbos. Your best run is saved as a **ghost**, so next time you race against yourself!
- **ONLINE** 🌍: race friends on other computers (see below).

## Build your hover ride

Pick a **BODY**, an **ENGINE** and **FINS**. Every part changes your stats (speed, acceleration,
weight, handling, grip and mini-turbo). You can paint it any color.

- **Bodies**: Hover Kart, Hover Bike, Sky Board, Baby Buggy, Sigma Chopper, Turbo Jet, UFO Saucer, Mega Mech, Speed Sled, Gold Sigma Kart
- **Engines**: Twin Jets, Ion Rings, Fan Turbines, Mega Thruster, Rocket Pack, Sigma Reactor
- **Fins**: Standard Fins, Spoiler Wing, Glider Wings, Para-Wing, Sigma Blades (they open up into wings when you glide!)

Some parts are 🔒 locked: **collect coins** in races to unlock them.

## Online (up to 4 players)

1. Everybody opens the game and clicks **ONLINE**, then picks a racer and a ride.
2. One player clicks **HOST A ROOM**. The game shows a **4-letter code** (like `KQZP`).
3. The other players type the code and click **JOIN**.
4. The host picks the track, the speed and if there are CPUs and items, then clicks **START RACE!**

Every computer needs **internet**. The game uses a free service called PeerJS to find each other,
and then the computers talk directly. If it doesn't connect, try Chrome or Edge on all computers.

## Make your own track! 🛠️

Open **`hover-gp/js/tracks.js`**. Every track is a list of pieces, driven one after the other:

```js
course: [
  'straight 120 items',      // drive straight 120 meters, with a row of item boxes
  'right 90 45 bank 8',      // turn right 90 degrees, 45 m wide, tilted
  'straight 80 boost coins', // a boost pad and a line of coins
  'jump 28',                 // a ramp and a 28 m hole to fly over!
  'loop',                    // a loop-the-loop
  'left 90 50',
],
```

All the words you can use are written at the top of the file (`up 10`, `down 10`, `glide 80`, `twist 100`,
`fall` (no walls!), `tunnel`, `water`, `ice`, `hazard:crusher`...). Save, reload the page, and your new track is there.
Don't worry if the end doesn't meet the start: the game bends the track a little to make it fit.

Want to see every racer and vehicle part? Open **`hover-gp/tools/garage.html`**.

## Graphics

If the game is slow, open **SETTINGS** and set **GRAPHICS: LOW** (then reload the page).

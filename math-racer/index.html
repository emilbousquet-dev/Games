Math Racer

Drive through the right answer!

For teachers

Math Racer is a quick math practice game. Each race has 10 questions. Kids drive the car into the lane with the right answer and get up to 3 stars.

  • Easy: adding up to 10
  • Medium: adding and taking away
  • Hard: times tables

Wrong answers show the right one, so kids learn as they go. Controls: arrow keys, or tap above or below the car.

There is also a hidden Game Center with fun games, opened with a code.

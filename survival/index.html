Surviving

🍖 Eat and 💧 drink or you get weak. Drink from the lake or the river, pick berries, search houses for food.

🌳 Punch trees for wood, then craft a Stone Axe and a Pickaxe. Everything you can make is in the CRAFT list.

🌙 At night zombies are faster and there are more of them. Build walls and a door, put spikes outside, and light a campfire.

🛏 Build a Bed: when you die, you wake up there. Your backpack stays where you died.

☠ Every 5th night, a HORDE attacks, led by a giant HORDE BOSS. Beat it for great loot!

⭐ Kill zombies, chop trees and survive nights to get XP. Every level gives you more health and stronger hits.

📦 Every day at noon a plane drops a SUPPLY CRATE. Follow the 📦 on your compass (and watch out, the noise brings zombies).

🐕 Three dogs are hiding in the world (look at the map). Press E to make friends. Your dog follows you and bites zombies!

🧨 Throw firecrackers: zombies run to the noise... then BOOM.

Escape

📡 Find the 3 RADIO PARTS (police station, gas station, hunter's cabin), bring them to the radio tower on Radio Hill, survive one last night... and get on the rescue helicopter!

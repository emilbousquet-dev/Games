HOW TO PLAY

AUTO PARKOURYour runner runs, jumps, climbs, wall runs, slides and grabs ziplines by himself!
SteerMove the mouse left and right (or slide your finger anywhere on the screen, or A D). Grab coins and dodge missiles!
🚀 RocketPress the ROCKET button (or click, or F). Rockets fly to the helicopter by themselves!
Σ Sigma modeDo tricks to fill the Σ bar, then press the SIGMA button (or E)
PauseEsc or P · M mutes the sound
Want to do every move yourself?Pick MANUAL controls in SETTINGS
RunYou run by yourself! (hold S to stop)
SteerMove the mouse, or A D to step sideways, or ← → to turn
